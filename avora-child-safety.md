# AVORA — Child Safety Standards

**Effective date:** 22 August 2026

AVORA is committed to protecting children and preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM) on its service.

## Zero-tolerance standard
AVORA prohibits any content, conduct, account, room, message, profile, image, video, audio, link, solicitation, grooming behavior, sexualization of minors, trafficking, sextortion, or other activity that exploits or endangers children. CSAM and attempts to obtain, share, promote, trade, or facilitate CSAM are strictly prohibited.

## User reporting
AVORA provides in-app reporting tools so users can report profiles and unsafe or policy-violating content or behavior. Reports are sent to the moderation process for review. Users should report suspected child-safety violations immediately and should not redistribute suspected illegal material.

## Enforcement
AVORA may remove content, restrict features, suspend or terminate accounts, preserve relevant evidence where legally required, and take other protective action when child-safety violations are identified. Serious or credible violations may be escalated to appropriate regional or national authorities or child-safety organizations as required by applicable law.

## CSAM response
AVORA does not permit CSAM. When AVORA becomes aware of apparent CSAM or child sexual exploitation, it will take appropriate action consistent with applicable law, including disabling access, preserving information where required, and making legally required reports to the relevant authorities.

## Compliance
AVORA is committed to complying with applicable child-safety laws and Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy for social and community applications.
