Delete Your AVORA Account

AVORA users may request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

In-App Method

When account deletion is available in the released application, open AVORA and navigate to Settings > Account > Delete Account and follow the confirmation process.

Support Method

If you cannot access your account, contact AVORA through its official support channel and request account deletion. Identity or account ownership verification may be required to protect the account from unauthorized deletion.

What Is Deleted

Personal account information and associated data will be deleted or anonymized as applicable, except information that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, transaction-record or dispute-resolution obligations.

Processing

Deletion requests will be processed within the period required by applicable law and platform requirements.
