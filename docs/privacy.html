AVORA Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

AVORA is a social voice, messaging and entertainment platform. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, protected and managed when using AVORA.

Information We May Collect

Depending on the features used, AVORA may process account and profile information, user-generated content, messages, voice-room activity, device and technical information, transaction records, safety and moderation information, and information voluntarily provided by users.

Permissions

AVORA may request permissions such as microphone, camera, photos/media, notifications or other device permissions only when required for relevant features. Permission availability does not mean information is collected continuously.

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide and improve AVORA, operate social and communication features, maintain account security, prevent fraud and abuse, process transactions, enforce platform rules, provide customer support and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Safety and Moderation

AVORA may process reports, content and relevant activity to detect abuse, fraud, harmful behavior or policy violations. Automated systems may assist moderation, but appropriate review and safeguards may also be used.

Data Sharing

AVORA does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to operate the service, protect users, process authorized transactions, comply with law, or with the user's direction or consent.

Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary for service, security, legal, fraud-prevention and legitimate operational purposes. Users may request account deletion and deletion of associated personal data, subject to legally required or security-related retention.

Children and Age Requirements

AVORA will apply age requirements and protections appropriate to its services and applicable laws. Features may be restricted according to age, region or platform requirements.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as AVORA develops. Material changes will be reflected on this page or communicated where required.

Contact

Privacy and support requests can be submitted through AVORA's official support channels.
